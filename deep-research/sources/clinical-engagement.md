# Clinical goals, engagement, alliance and timing: source checks

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/meta-review-cbt/blob/main/deep-research/sources/clinical-engagement.md>

**TLDR:** Research supports acceptance-based goals, alliance–outcome associations, adapting directiveness to reactance, and structured outcome feedback. It does not validate the proposed four-type classification, establish its prevalence, or show that cognitive techniques must always follow experience.

## Timing counterevidence (cross-topic sources)

See [`evolutionary-learning.md`](evolutionary-learning.md) for the full source notes and retrieval records.

- **Krause et al. (2022)**, `10.1177/01454455221075754`: full-text methods/results read; 93 randomized, one-month follow-up. No detected outcome difference between restructuring-before and consolidation-after exposure. Cognitive content differed too, and the trial did not establish equivalence. The sample did not require the diagnostic functional-impairment criterion.
- **Johnco et al. (2025)**, `10.1037/ccp0000953`: abstract read; 249 adults with elevated public-speaking anxiety, single session and one-week follow-up. Restructuring-before and behavioural experiments both exceeded exposure without explicit expectancy processing. The small, non-significant anxiety difference favoured behavioural experiments, which also produced greater expectancy change in a shorter session. This is counterevidence to a universal rule against cognitive work before exposure, not a full clinical test of relational readiness; it also retains the favourable direction for processing after exposure.

## What was not verified

I did not find a validated version of the exact four-type classification in the two targeted Europe PMC typology queries or the reviewed readiness/reactance sources. I also did not find a direct test of “early restructuring causes resistance specifically because the alliance is not established” in the targeted timing/alliance/resistance query and the sources above. Exact query URLs, retrieval dates and returned titles are in [`targeted_search_receipts.json`](../../experiments/03_clinical_goals_engagement_and_timing/sources/targeted_search_receipts.json).

The searches were narrow and not exhaustive. Related clinical taxonomies may exist; the absence of a match here is not proof of global absence. No prevalence claim for the four situations is retained. The manuscript labels the perspective informal, rephrases it as formulation questions rather than patient labels, and infers no diagnosis from disengagement.

**TLDR-end:** [meta-review-cbt: clinical-engagement evidence] The clinical observations connect to research, but supporting related constructs does not validate the four-type scheme or a universal experience-first mechanism. Acceptance, delivery and timing qualify the thesis rather than proving cognition useless.
