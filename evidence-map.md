# Why CBT can fail: evidence and gaps

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/meta-review-cbt/blob/main/evidence-map.md>

**TLDR:** The evidence supports specific criticisms of cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), including incomplete response, comparator-sensitive effects and uncertain component mechanisms. It also reports benefits that a credible critique must explain. This is a targeted seed map, not a completed systematic meta-review.

## The argument and what would establish it

| Criticism | Retrieved evidence | What is justified / what remains open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Many people do not obtain the intended benefit. | [Loerinc et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26319194/) systematically examined anxiety response definitions and found response estimates depend on their definition and analysis population. | Nonresponse belongs at the center of the review. Response, remission, meaningful functioning and deterioration must be extracted separately; a nonresponder is not automatically harmed. |
| The apparent benefit depends on what CBT is compared with. | [Cuijpers et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2025.0482) found control-dependent effects across adult conditions. [Carpenter et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29451967/) found benefit against psychological/pill placebo for anxiety. | Waitlist contrasts do not establish superiority to credible treatment. Placebo-controlled benefit is counterevidence to universal ineffectiveness. |
| A successful package does not validate its cognitive explanation. | [Jacobson et al., 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8871414/) randomized depressed adults to behavioral activation, activation plus automatic-thought work, or full cognitive therapy; the full package did not show better outcomes. | Challenges the necessity of the full package in that comparison. It is one component trial, not proof that cognitive work never adds value or that nonsignificant treatments are equivalent. |
| CBT may not be uniquely superior to alternatives. | [Cuijpers et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/wps.21069) found a small advantage over other psychotherapies for depression that was not robust in most sensitivity analyses. | “Best established” and “best for everyone” are different claims. The result does not establish that mindfulness, medication or any particular alternative is always better. |
| Research design may overstate practical benefit. | [Carpenter et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29451967/) reported larger effects in completers than intention-to-treat samples. [Cuijpers et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2025.0482) identifies trial-quality, heterogeneity and publication-bias limitations. | Extract missingness, risk of bias, selective reporting, measurement, and comparator-specific estimates. Do not treat bias adjustment as a uniquely correct causal estimate. |
| Cognitive restructuring may demand capacities weakened by distress. | [Arnsten, 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19455173/) reviews stress-related prefrontal impairment. [Johnco et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2014.04.005) examines flexibility and restructuring in older adults; use the bibliography for verified article metadata. | Mechanistic motivation, not a demonstrated cross-condition CBT failure mechanism. Baseline predictor and treatment-interaction tests are required; executive function measured after treatment does not answer the question. |
| Short-term symptom change may not establish durable recovery. | The [2025 cross-condition synthesis](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2025.0482) focused on post-treatment outcomes. The [2023 depression synthesis](https://doi.org/10.1002/wps.21069) also examined follow-up and reported sustained benefit against controls. | Audit relapse, functioning and follow-up attrition condition by condition. A claim that all benefit disappears would contradict retrieved evidence. |
| Burden, dropout and harms may be missed by average symptom scores. | The [2025 synthesis](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2025.0482) reports dropout differences across conditions; the [2018 anxiety synthesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29451967/) reports excess dropout for CBT in its posttraumatic-stress subset. | Dropout is an acceptability signal, not itself proof of harm or its cause. Deterioration, adverse events, opportunity costs, accessibility and real-world implementation need dedicated retrieval. |

## How the original drafts guide the review

The [blog](sources/blog-original.md), [Google Docs version](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-3ynnqjyrLw-rwftcR6Rpa1h7bhbhCDThq4cZlmU0bI/edit), and [prior critical review](sources/deep-research-draft-2.pdf) motivate the cognitive-demand and component arguments. The current manuscript retains that skeptical direction while making the supporting evidence and its limits explicit. The draft's metaphor that the prefrontal cortex goes “offline” is not a literal demonstration that reasoning or learning becomes impossible.

## Coverage and next extraction

The direct executive-function question in adult attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder is one [21-record case study](data/s03-screening.md). It cannot settle the broader question. No new cross-condition effect has been pooled here. Effect sizes, confidence intervals, analysis populations and follow-up denominators must be extracted before numerical comparative verdicts. Current seed studies were retrieved by targeted lookup; systematic searches across all condition strata and independent screening remain pending.
