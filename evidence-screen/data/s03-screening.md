# ADHD case study: 21 executive-function hits screened

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/meta-review-cbt/blob/main/evidence-screen/data/s03-screening.md>

**TLDR:** No direct baseline executive-function predictor or moderator test is confirmed in the material retrieved for these 21 records. Sixteen records can presently be classified as not direct tests; five candidate full texts remain unresolved. This is preliminary single-agent screening, not a completed two-reviewer systematic assessment or evidence that no such study exists.

## What counts as a direct test

The study must measure executive function before treatment and test its relationship to subsequent CBT clinical/functional outcome. Prediction is a prognostic association; moderation requires a baseline-EF-by-treatment interaction. Measuring EF improvement, comparing group-by-time slopes, mediation by change, or classifying emotion regulation is not sufficient.

The exact S03 query was re-exported on 10-21-2026 with 21 core records, agreeing with the earlier count. The original count receipt retained only one identifier, so the earlier complete membership is not independently frozen. See [retrieval receipt](s03-retrieval.json) and [machine-readable decisions](s03-screening.csv). Counts describe records, not unique independent trials; no confidence interval or significance test applies.

## Most relevant unresolved evidence

The emotion-regulation subphenotype paper (S03-09) is an indirect candidate: its profiles are constructed from emotion-regulation instruments, and EF differs across those profiles. A profile-associated response is not automatically baseline EF predicting or moderating CBT. S03-08, S03-11, S03-12 and S03-20 require full methods/results/supplements to rule in or rule out an additional baseline analysis. Their abstracts mainly describe EF outcomes or mediation. No interaction estimate, confidence interval, p-value or confirmed baseline-EF test is available for these five from the material assessed.

## Report overlap and discrepancies

The OSF primary PDFs retrieved for S03-01 and S03-02 have exactly the same SHA-256 hash (see CSV) despite different indexed descriptions. S03-03 also describes a matching 674-person setting but a different allocation account. These are linked pending provenance clarification, not counted as independent trials. The same PDF in two repository records does not establish which indexed design is correct. The scoping-review preprint S03-13 accompanies S03-10. Protocol/result pairs include S03-14/S03-12 and S03-18/S03-20.

## Implication for the paper

This search mainly surfaces outcomes, protocols, reviews and indirect profiles. It does not currently establish that baseline EF explains CBT failure in adult ADHD. Equally, it does not establish absence of moderation. Finish candidate full-text retrieval, inspect companion analyses and citation chains, and obtain independent screening before treating the case-study result as final. The broader project examines CBT across conditions and must not infer its overall verdict from this subset.
