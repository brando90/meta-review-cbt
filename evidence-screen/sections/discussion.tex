\section{Discussion: reasons for failure and limits of the critique}
\subsection{Nonresponse, comparators and distinctive value}
A review focused on failure must examine individual response and functioning rather than treating a favorable average as sufficient.
Anxiety response estimates depend on definitions and analysis populations \citep{Loerinc2015}.
Comparator-dependent results across conditions make the choice between waitlist, usual care and credible alternatives central to interpretation \citep{Cuijpers2025}.
For depression, limited robustness of superiority over other psychotherapies supports caution about claims of unique comparative advantage, while the same synthesis reports benefit against controls and at follow-up \citep{Cuijpers2023}.

\subsection{Component explanations and executive demands}
Package efficacy does not establish that changing thoughts through deliberate reasoning is necessary for benefit.
The depression component trial challenges that inference for its tested comparisons, but a nonsignificant advantage does not prove equivalence or universal dispensability of cognitive work \citep{Jacobson1996}.
Likewise, stress-related prefrontal impairment motivates an executive-demand hypothesis without directly testing CBT outcomes \citep{Arnsten2009}.
The baseline predictor, treatment moderator and post-treatment outcome remain separate evidence categories \citep{Kraemer2002}.
The preliminary ADHD screen cannot close this question while candidate full texts remain unresolved.

\subsection{Bias, durability, burden and harms}
Completer analyses can differ from intention-to-treat estimates in anxiety trials, so missingness and analysis populations require explicit extraction \citep{Carpenter2018}.
The cross-condition synthesis identifies limitations in study quality, heterogeneity and publication bias, and its focus on post-treatment outcomes leaves longer-term questions open \citep{Cuijpers2025}.
Dropout may indicate acceptability problems, but does not identify harm or its cause.
Dedicated searches for deterioration, adverse events, opportunity costs and real-world access are still needed; these are research questions rather than established universal failures.

\subsection{What the current review can claim}
The retrieved evidence justifies specific criticisms and a broader research program; it also includes evidence of benefit that the argument must accommodate \citep{Carpenter2018,Cuijpers2023,Cuijpers2025}.
The prior author drafts are preserved as the source of the skeptical argument, not substituted for a completed systematic review.
The current seed map has incomplete coverage, and the case-study screening is preliminary and conducted by one agent.
No pooled cross-condition treatment effect, universal efficacy verdict or individual treatment recommendation follows from this draft.
