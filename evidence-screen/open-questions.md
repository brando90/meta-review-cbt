## Broader review priorities

1. Execute systematic cross-condition searches, including nonresponse, mechanisms/components, comparator bias, durability, harms and implementation; targeted seeds are not full coverage.
2. Extract condition-specific effects and uncertainty, distinguishing passive controls from credible alternatives and response from remission/functioning.
3. Test the blog's proposed cognitive-demand and component explanations against contrary evidence; do not assume mindfulness or medication is superior without direct comparisons.
4. Resolve the five ADHD candidate full texts and the inconsistent preprint records; obtain an independent screen.

## Scope safeguards

No claim that CBT is universally effective, ineffective or appropriate for an individual follows from this repository. A plausible mechanism critique is not a demonstrated treatment-effect moderator. Conversely, a mean symptom benefit does not establish equal benefit at every executive-function level. Both questions require direct evidence.
