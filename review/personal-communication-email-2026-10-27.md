## Proposed contribution (for the review's framing)
1. Distinguish intervention **format** from the **mechanism** it engages (a verbal intervention can invite an emotional experience; an action exercise can change beliefs).
2. Examine when emotionally meaningful experiences help patients update expectations and reduce avoidance, and how cognitive interventions prepare, interpret and consolidate that learning.
3. Do not read "bottom-up" as a proven, exclusively subcortical mechanism. Emotional intensity alone is not evidence of change. Findings do not validate brief strategic therapy without studies that test it.
