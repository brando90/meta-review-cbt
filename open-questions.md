## Scope safeguards

No claim that CBT is universally effective, ineffective or appropriate for an individual follows from this repository. A plausible mechanism critique is not a demonstrated treatment-effect moderator. Conversely, a mean symptom benefit does not establish equal benefit at every executive-function level. Both questions require direct evidence.
