## How the sources are used

The blog motivates the hypothesis that cognitive restructuring imposes executive demands that may matter for some people with adult attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD). The broader review covers anxiety, stress, treatment components and alternative interventions. The current project restores that broader critical scope. Adult ADHD and direct executive-function predictor/moderator studies form one case study, not the boundary of the review. The original blog and Google Docs version remain unchanged.

The PDF describes itself as a critical theoretical review, **not a completed systematic review**. Its statement that about 300 sources were identifier-verified is an imported author claim; the underlying verification ledger was not retrieved. Identifier resolution alone would not establish that every clinical claim is supported. The new `references.bib` contains only independently retrieved references used in the current manuscript. Citation and claim checks for that manuscript are recorded in `../data/citation-evidence.csv`.

The PDF's journal-style “Preprint submitted to” footer is part of the imported artifact, not evidence of submission. No journal submission is made by this initialization. No unrelated clinical records or correspondence are included.
