\section{Methods and protocol}
\label{sec:methods}
Protocol version 0.2, dated October 21, 2026, restores the author's cross-condition scope and has not been registered.
The full operational amendment is maintained in \texttt{protocol.md}.
The primary stream includes systematic reviews and meta-analyses of CBT in adults across clinical conditions, with each condition and comparator retained separately.
Supplementary streams examine component trials, moderators, mechanisms, harms and implementation.
Outcomes include symptoms, response, remission, functioning, quality of life, durability, deterioration, dropout and burden.
The initial ten-query search and the executive-function screening exercise are an ADHD case study, not the primary review's population restriction.

For that case study, eligible populations are adults with diagnosed ADHD, and mixed samples require separately extractable findings.
Intervention components and delivery formats are recorded separately.
Comparators are classified as passive, active psychological, or medication-only for adjunctive treatment.
Across conditions, the meta-review includes systematic reviews with explicit search and selection methods.
The ADHD baseline-executive-function stream requires pretreatment executive-function measurement and a later outcome association or treatment interaction.
Single-arm associations are prognostic evidence, not causal treatment-effect moderation.
Post-treatment executive-function scores alone do not enter the baseline moderator synthesis.
We will record whether measures are performance-based or self-reported, as well as diagnosis, medication, comorbidity, outcome overlap, missingness and prespecification.

Reviews will be appraised using AMSTAR 2, with domain judgments retained \citep{Shea2017}.
The original-study risk-of-bias instrument will be fixed in a protocol amendment before extraction.
Review overlap will be mapped at the primary-study level; overlapping meta-analyses will not be pooled as independent estimates.
Quantitative moderation synthesis remains conditional on compatible constructs, comparisons and interaction estimands, with a statistical analysis amendment required before fitting.
