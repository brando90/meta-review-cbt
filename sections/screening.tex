\section{Screening and extraction plan}
Two independent reviewers will pilot the eligibility rules and screen titles/abstracts and full texts, with an adjudicator resolving remaining disagreements.
Reviewers have not yet been assigned, and no independent screening is claimed in this draft.
Full-text exclusions will receive explicit reasons; unavailable texts will remain awaiting retrieval.
Companion reports will be linked to underlying studies, and duplicates across databases and reviews will be tracked.

Extraction will record outcome-specific denominators, intervention components, executive-function timing and instrument, comparison group, rater, follow-up, estimate, confidence interval, reported significance test, covariates, missingness and prespecification.
One-arm associations, between-arm interactions and changes in executive function will be labeled separately.
The selection flow will be populated only after screening, following PRISMA reporting guidance \citep{Page2021}.
