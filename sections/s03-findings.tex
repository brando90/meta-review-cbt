\subsection{Preliminary screening of 21 executive-function records}
A full export of the ADHD executive-function/predictor slice returned 21 records on October 21, 2026.
Single-agent screening confirmed no direct baseline executive-function predictor or treatment-moderator test in the retrieved material.
Sixteen records were classified as not direct tests, while five candidate full texts remain unresolved.
These are record-level descriptive counts, not unique-trial totals or inferential estimates; confidence intervals and significance tests do not apply.
The full titles, identifiers, evidence levels, reasons and source locations appear in \texttt{data/s03-screening.csv} and its Markdown companion.

The unresolved candidates have PubMed identifiers 39798484, 41962667, 41220055, 33615398 and 28866911.
The emotion-regulation profile report (41962667) is indirect: its grouping variables are not baseline executive-function measures, and its full outcome model remains to be assessed.
The other abstracts mainly describe executive-function outcomes or mediation rather than direct baseline prediction.
Absence from an abstract does not establish absence from the full study.

Two preprint records yielded byte-identical primary PDFs despite differing indexed descriptions; another has an apparently overlapping sample and a different allocation description.
They are flagged for provenance clarification and cannot be treated as independent replications.
Protocols and companion publications are also linked in the ledger.
This provisional screen is evidence of a retrieval and classification gap, not evidence that baseline executive function never matters or that CBT fails generally.
