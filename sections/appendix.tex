\section{Source archive and reproducibility}
\label{sec:appendix}
The repository preserves the author's original blog Markdown, a Google document text export, and the 29-page deep-research Draft 2 PDF with extracted text.
Source identifiers and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in \texttt{sources/manifest.json}.
The imported review motivates the broader anxiety and cognitive-restructuring question and identifies itself as a critical theoretical review rather than a completed systematic review.
Its claim of approximately 300 identifier-verified sources is not independently certified here; the underlying ledger was not retrieved.
Its journal-style submission footer is not evidence of an actual submission.

The current bibliography uses separately retrieved Europe PMC metadata, with a claim-and-scope ledger in \texttt{data/citation-evidence.csv}.
Metadata and abstract checks establish source identity and support the limited statements used here; they do not replace full-text assessment for the review.
Run \texttt{make test} for deterministic checks and \texttt{make paper} to compile this manuscript with latexmk and BibTeX.
Search reruns use a new run label and retain earlier receipts.
The public repository is \url{https://github.com/brando90/meta-review-cbt}.
