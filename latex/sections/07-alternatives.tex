\section{Alternatives and their evidence}\label{sec:alternatives}
\subsection{Mindfulness and acceptance-based treatments}
The alternative that follows from the argument is to train attention and acceptance directly instead of disputing thoughts. Brewer frames anxiety as a habit loop (trigger, worry, a feeling of control) and proposes noticing, curiosity about the reward value of worry, and a ``bigger, better offer'' in place of argument \citep{brewer2021unwinding}. Evidence for this specific programme is early: in a randomised trial of an app-based programme for generalised anxiety ($n=65$), anxiety scores fell by 67\% versus 14\% under usual care, with mediation analyses implicating reduced worry \citep{roy2021app}. The trial is small, uses self-report, compares with usual care rather than CBT, and has a developer among the authors.

Against evidence-based comparators, mindfulness-based programmes do not outperform them: a meta-analysis found essentially no difference (Hedges' $g\approx 0.00$) against evidence-based treatments, often CBT \citep{goldberg2018mbi}, and acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT) versus CBT shows the same pattern \citep{atjak2015act}. In a trial of 276 adults with anxiety disorders, an eight-week mindfulness-based stress reduction course was noninferior to escitalopram, with fewer discontinuations for adverse events \citep{hoge2023mbsr}. That is parity with a first-line drug, not superiority to CBT. In adult ADHD, mindfulness-based cognitive therapy added to usual care showed a benefit \citep[$d=0.41$;][]{janssen2019mbct}, while a later meta-analysis found only small effects against active controls \citep{oliva2021mbi}.

\subsection{Medication}
In a network meta-analysis of ADHD medications, stimulants outperformed placebo in adults, with standardised effects of about $0.8$ (amphetamines) and $0.5$ (methylphenidate) on clinician ratings, over short follow-up of about 12 weeks \citep{cortese2018adhdmeds}. If baseline executive capacity is the bottleneck, raising it pharmacologically before layering skills is mechanistically coherent, but this review does not test that combination.
