\section{Does executive function moderate CBT outcome?}\label{sec:moderators}
This is the question on which the argument stands or falls, and the direct evidence is thin. In a pilot study of anxious older adults, participants whose executive function remained low did not respond to CBT, while those whose executive function improved did \citep{mohlman2005ef}. In another sample, low cognitive flexibility did not predict overall CBT outcome but did predict less acquisition of cognitive-restructuring skill specifically \citep{johnco2014flex}; the second result is the closest published evidence to the claim made here. Both studies are in older adults, are small, and cut in different directions on overall outcome. We did not find an adequately powered study of working-age adults with ADHD and anxiety.
